# Sales Performance Semantic Layer

Your partner for clear business numbers.

## What is the Semantic Layer?

A single source of truth that lets everyone get the same trusted answers fast.

- See the same revenue numbers the whole company uses
- No more asking the data team for the "real" number
- Answers ready in chat, dashboards, and reports

## Key Metrics

| Metric | In Plain English | Why You Care |
|--------|------------------|--------------|
| total_revenue | Total money from every completed order | Tells you the overall size of the business |
| order_count | How many orders customers placed | Shows how busy your sales are |

## Dimensions

You can break numbers down by:

- Time (day, month, quarter)
- Product category
- Region

## Common Questions This Answers

- What was our revenue last month?
- Which product category grew the fastest?
- How many orders came from the West region?

## Glossary

**Revenue**: The total money received from sales.

**Order**: A completed purchase by a customer.
